Home

Rebuilt on Kelli Marie’s section order, because that page is a belief-change sequence rather than a menu. Nine blocks, top to bottom, each one changing what the reader believes before the next one asks for anything.

Why this order works. Her homepage never explains what you get or what it costs — it defers every logistic to the Info page and spends the homepage making you feel this is for someone like you. It also runs a descending CTA ladder: Book a session at the top, then Meet the Team, then Schedule a call, then a newsletter at the bottom. Four different levels of readiness, four different doors.

What I changed for you. One insertion — your session fee, at block 6. She shows no pricing anywhere. You need the $697 and the inclusions on the page because they qualify people before they reach your calendar, and your Tuesday/Thursday grid is too narrow to spend on calls that were never going to book.

Two blocks need your words, not mine. Block 3 works because the photographer discloses something real before asking the reader to. I have drafted it from what your About page already says — the social work, the farm, boudoir being all you shoot — but the deeper personal beat is yours and I will not invent it. Block 7 needs your actual Google reviews; I am not writing testimonials.

Brand note

Worth saying out loud, because it will shape whatever you do next.

You are running two visual identities. The Showit site is white, black and blush #ffe4dc, set in Nanum Myeongjo, Rufina and Montserrat — the template's palette, not a chosen one. Your Scalogy pages are a dark studio palette: near-black #13100E, warm ink #F2ECE2, antique gold #C9A24B, set in Cormorant Garamond and Jost.

The second one is considerably better, and it's the one a client sees at checkout, at her reveal booking, and on her payment plan. It reads like a $9,400 collection. The blush template reads like a starter site.

This kit is deliberately scoped to copy and plumbing — a visual overhaul is a separate, bigger job. But when you're ready, the direction already exists. You built it. The page you're reading is set in it.
