# The Google reviews, and where each one sits

Nine reviews, six places on the site they can go, and **no review appears
in two of them**. That rule is in `CLAUDE.md`; this file is the register
that makes it checkable.

Jessica sent the last five on 24 September. Text is verbatim from Google
except for typographic entities (`&ldquo;`, `&rsquo;`) and, where noted,
an existing light edit that predates this file.

## Placed

| Where | Signed | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| Home, first of three | Miss J. | "This was a very great experience!..." |
| Home, second | Miss S. | "Jessica was absolutely amazing!..." |
| Home, third | Miss M. | "I couldn't have been happier..." |
| Portfolio pull-quote | Miss R. | "Jess is amazing to work with!..." |
| FAQ pull-quote | Miss A. | "Jessica is great to work with..." |
| The Experience pull-quote | Miss N. | "Jessica was fabulous from the very first contact..." |

Why those three, and not others:

- **Portfolio — Miss R.** The portfolio is where a stranger has just
  scrolled twelve women's galleries thinking every one of them was braver
  than she is. Miss R. wrote "this was my first boudoir shoot and I was so
  nervous" and answers that thought head on.
- **FAQ — Miss A.** She names the pampering of hair and makeup, the shoot
  and the one-on-one photo selection, in that order: the running order the
  answers above her explain.
- **The Experience — Miss N.** "From the very first contact to the image
  reveal" is the arc that page walks step by step. It was already there and
  did not need moving.
/contact has no review. It carried Miss H.'s from the morning of
24 September until that afternoon, when Jessica cut the page back to her own
copy and the form -- it was 753px of a 3941px page, below the form where
proof does nothing, and 430px of it was a portrait the phone crop was
slicing through. Miss H. is unplaced again and is in the held list below,
not lost.

Miss M.'s was in three slots at once -- home, portfolio and FAQ -- beside
three different faces, which reads as three women who happened to write the
identical paragraph. It is now on the home page only.

## Held, with nowhere to put them yet

Two reviews are unused, and both are held for the same reason rather than for
want of a slot. The obvious remaining slot is an About pull-quote: that page
still carries no review at all.

> "Oh my gosh Jessica is Amazing! She makes you feel so comfortable and
> beautiful in your own skin. Really she is so professional and fun and I
> will definitely be booking another session with her. You will feel so
> good about yourself when you are done!"
> -- **Miss K.**

> "Jessica is absolutely fabulous to work with! This was an amazing
> experience! Such a confidence booster. She is very professional and it was
> such a fun and easy process. Thank you!!!"
> -- **Miss K.**

**Two different women both sign as Miss K.** On the portfolio that is fine
-- there are two Miss K galleries and they are plainly two sessions -- but
two reviews signed identically read as one woman posting twice, which is the
exact thing the one-review-one-page rule exists to prevent. Both are held
until Jessica says how to tell them apart. Neither is on the site.

> "Jessica is amazing! Such a lovely day. She is so fun and professional!
> She really makes the studio a safe and comfortable place! Recommend to
> anyone who wants to feel good about themselves or just have a fun
> self-love day!"
> -- **Miss H.**

**Miss H. is held for want of a page, not for want of merit.** Hers is the
only review on the site that talks about the room itself, which makes it the
natural one for a page about walking into the studio. It was on /contact for
a few hours on 24 September and came off with everything else when that page
was cut back. The Experience is where it would go next if Miss N. ever
moves.

## Things this register is for

- Nothing is retyped from memory. Add a review here first, then place it.
- Before adding a review to a page, check this table. Before changing a
  page's review, change this table.
- The photograph beside a pull-quote is **not** the woman who wrote it and
  carries no name for that reason. That is stated in the CSS comment the
  pages share, and it is why a review can move pages without the frame
  moving with it.
