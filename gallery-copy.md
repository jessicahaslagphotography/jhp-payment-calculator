# Gallery copy — DRAFT, for Jessica to approve

Twelve gallery pages run about **129 words** each and the portfolio index
about **220**. To Google that reads as thin, near-duplicate content: twelve
pages that look like the same page with different photographs on it. It is
the last item on the SEO list and the only one that cannot be fixed with
markup, because what is missing is words.

**Nothing here is on the site yet.** This is a draft. Approve it, change it,
or bin it — it is your copy, and the house rule is that your copy is yours.
Once you say go it is one change to `build-gallery.py`, one rebuild and one
push.

---

## How it sits on the page

Two blocks per gallery, not one wall of text:

    <h1>Miss K</h1>
    LEDE          one or two sentences, under the name, above the frames
    [ the photographs ]
    CLOSE         two short paragraphs, under the frames, above
                  Previous / All sessions / Next
    [ Your Turn band ]

A visitor gets a sentence of context before the work and the rest after it,
which is the order that respects the photographs. Google reads both.

Word count per page goes from about **129 to about 265**. That is not a long
page, and it should not be — it is a gallery. It is enough to stop being a
duplicate.

---

## Where every fact in this draft came from

Everything specific is **read off the photographs themselves** — the alt-text
pass on 26 September described all 165 frames, so I know what is in every one
of them: the wardrobe, the setting, the light, the props. Counts of setups and
looks are counted, not estimated.

**Nothing here invents anything about a client.** Not why she booked, not how
she felt, not her age, not her occasion, not whether she was nervous. I do not
know any of that and neither does the camera. Where a sentence needed a human
being in it, it is about *you*, the reader, or about how I work — never a claim
about the woman in the frame.

**No figures.** Not the session fee, not the session length, not the reveal
window. Every one of those already lives in the FAQ, and one number in
thirteen places is thirteen chances to go stale — which is exactly how the old
Canva guide came to undercut the site by $197.

**The location is in each one, once**, phrased differently each time, because
that is the local-search signal these pages are currently missing.

# Two things to decide, both yours

## A · The duplicate titles — this one is a real bug

`portfolio-miss-k` and `portfolio-miss-k-2` currently carry **byte-identical
`<title>` tags**:

    Miss K — A Boudoir Session | JHP Boudoir
    Miss K — A Boudoir Session | JHP Boudoir

So do the two Miss S pages. Google treats identical titles on different URLs as
a signal that one of them is redundant, and picks one to show. Four pages, two
titles, two of them competing with each other.

The copy above fixes the descriptions on its own. The titles need a short label
each. My suggestion, two or three words, also usable as the gold kicker above
the name on the page:

| page | label | resulting title | chars |
|---|---|---|---|
| miss-k | Candlelight and Boots | Miss K — Candlelight and Boots \| Boudoir, Jefferson City MO | 59 |
| miss-k-2 | The Library | Miss K — The Library \| Boudoir, Jefferson City MO | 49 |
| miss-a | Feathers and Lamplight | Miss A — Feathers and Lamplight \| Boudoir, Jefferson City MO | 60 |
| miss-s | Black Lace and Red | Miss S — Black Lace and Red \| Boudoir, Jefferson City MO | 56 |
| miss-s-2 | Roses and Purple Satin | Miss S — Roses and Purple Satin \| Boudoir, Jefferson City MO | 60 |
| miss-t | Navy Lace | Miss T — Navy Lace \| Boudoir, Jefferson City MO | 47 |
| miss-j | Green Lace and Red Heels | Miss J — Green Lace and Red Heels \| Boudoir, Jefferson City MO | 62 |
| miss-p | Silver and Emerald Tulle | Miss P — Silver and Emerald Tulle \| Boudoir, Jefferson City MO | 62 |
| miss-e | Green Satin and Candles | Miss E — Green Satin and Candles \| Boudoir, Jefferson City MO | 61 |
| miss-v | The Cowboy Hat | Miss V — The Cowboy Hat \| Boudoir, Jefferson City MO | 52 |
| miss-m | Gold and Copper | Miss M — Gold and Copper \| Boudoir, Jefferson City MO | 53 |
| miss-h | Emerald Satin | Miss H — Emerald Satin \| Boudoir, Jefferson City MO | 51 |

Four of those run 60–62 characters and Google will clip the tail. Say the word
and I shorten the suffix to `| JHP Boudoir` on those four, which keeps every
one under 50 — the city is in the h1, the copy and the schema on every page
regardless.

**These are my words for your clients' sessions.** If "Candlelight and Boots"
is not how you would put it, rename any of them and I will use yours.

## B · Whether the labels appear on the page

The label can live only in the `<title>` — invisible, doing its job in search
results — or also as the small gold kicker above the name, the way the VIP band
uses one. On the page it reads:

    CANDLELIGHT AND BOOTS
    Miss K

That is a look, and it is a change to how every gallery opens. Your call, and
"title only" is a perfectly good answer.

# What this does not fix

The galleries stop being thin and stop being near-duplicates of each other.
They still have **no inbound links from anywhere**, which is the other half of
why a page ranks. The two things that would actually move these:

1. **The Google Business Profile** — still not touched, still about a third of
   local ranking weight on its own, still worth more than everything on this
   site put together.
2. **The blog** — five posts are waiting on Showit's reply about the WordPress
   login. A gallery page with a blog post linking into it is a different animal
   from a gallery page on its own.
