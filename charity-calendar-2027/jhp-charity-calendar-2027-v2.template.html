Good to know

Your questions, answered

Do I have to share my images publicly?

Nope! Entering the 2027 calendar is optional. You decide after you see your images at your image reveal and ordering appointment — and if you keep them private, you still get the $99 session and $500 off. A discount is a discount! These are YOUR images.

When would my session be?

Anytime between November 2026 and October 2027 — you pick the date that works for you.

How long are applications open?

For a limited time only. Once I have the applications I need, the application closes — so if you’re feeling it, apply now!

Which charity does the calendar support?

The charity will be chosen during the 2027 calendar promotion, and the calendar will be sold publicly to raise money for it.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. Applying is free and doesn’t commit you to anything. The $99 session fee only applies if you’re selected and choose to book.

How does the $500 off work?

Selected models take $500 off the package they purchase at their image reveal. There are 8 packages to choose from — they begin at $750 and include 3 digital images, and combo collections — an album, digitals and the mobile gallery app — are $2,900. Both prices already have your $500 discount applied. Images are purchased separately from the session fee.

I’ve never done anything like this. Can I still apply?

YES. Most of my clients have never been in front of a camera like this. I coach you through every single pose, and wardrobe malfunctions are totally normal — we fix it, laugh, and move on.

Is there a size, age or “look” requirement?

Absolutely not. Any body is a boudoir body. This calendar is about real women, exactly as they are.

Where are sessions held?

In my studio just outside Jefferson City, Missouri.
