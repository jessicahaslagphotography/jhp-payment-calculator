Meet your photographer

Hello, I’m Jess!

I was a social worker before I was a boudoir photographer. Years of sitting with people on the hardest day of their life taught me the thing that turned out to matter more than any camera setting: how to make a person feel safe, loved, and supported in a room.

I went into social work because I wanted to help people. I still do. It just looks nothing like what I pictured back then. Nobody warned me that the most useful thing I’d ever hand a woman would be a photograph of herself, and I wouldn’t trade it for the world.
