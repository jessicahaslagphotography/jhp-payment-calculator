Good to know

Your questions, answered

Which dates are included?

Every remaining session date on my calendar through December 31, 2026. I photograph on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and the list above shows what’s still open right now.

How long is this special running?

Until the dates are gone. Once every 2026 opening is booked, this round is over.

What does the $99 retainer cover?

It reserves your date and covers your optional consultation call, professional hair and makeup, private use of the studio, the client wardrobe, 60 minutes of photography with full posing coaching, and your private reveal appointment. It’s normally $697.

How does the discount work?

You choose your collection when you book. Full Collections (Artist, Diamond, Gold and Silver) are 40% off and start at $2,040. The Digital Gallery is 40% off too, at $3,000. Petite Collections (Petite 15, 8 and 3) are $500 off and start at $750. Sales tax is added at checkout on Full Collections because they include printed products; digital-only collections (the Digital Gallery and Petite) are tax-free.

When does my package need to be paid?

In full, 30 days before your session date, on an interest-free payment plan (weekly, biweekly or monthly). If your date is less than 30 days away, your package is paid in full when you book.

Can I upgrade at my reveal?

Absolutely. If you can’t narrow it down (most women can’t!), you can move up to a larger collection right there at your reveal.

I’ve never done anything like this. Is that okay?

YES. Most of my clients have never been in front of a camera like this. I coach you through every single pose and expression.

Where is the studio?

Just outside Jefferson City, Missouri: over 2,000 square feet built for boudoir, with a hair and makeup room, a wardrobe room and multiple sets.

The fine print
  • Last-minute opening pricing applies to sessions booked on remaining open dates through December 31, 2026, and is available until those dates are booked. Dates are first come, first served and are not held until the session fee retainer is paid.
  • The $99 session fee retainer is non-refundable and non-transferable. It reserves your date and covers your optional consultation call, professional hair and makeup, private studio time, client wardrobe access, 60 minutes of photography and your private reveal and ordering appointment.
  • Images and products are purchased separately from the session fee retainer. Full Collections are 40% off (from $2,040, regularly $3,400). The Digital Gallery is 40% off ($3,000, regularly $5,000). Petite Collections are $500 off (from $750, regularly $1,250). Prices shown do not include sales tax, which is applied at checkout to collections that include physical products; digital-only collections are not taxed. Discounts cannot be combined with any other offer or studio credit.
  • Your package must be paid in full no less than 30 days prior to your session date. Sessions booked less than 30 days out are paid in full at booking. Failure to complete payment results in session cancellation and forfeiture of the session fee retainer.
  • Clients must be 18 or older. No images are shared publicly without the client’s written permission. Last-minute openings cannot be rescheduled. Standard studio booking and cancellation policies apply.
