From the studio

Every age, every size, every kind of nervous

Feeling unsure? Read this first

Nervous? Good. You’re exactly who this is for.

“I won’t know how to pose.”

You won’t need to. I direct every pose, every hand, every chin tilt, out loud, the entire time.

“I’m not photogenic.”

Being photogenic is a skill I have, not one you need. Flattering light, posing chosen for your body, and an eye for your best angles — that’s my job.

“I don’t have the ‘right’ body.”

There is no such thing as the perfect body. You’re never too big, too small, too old or too young. Any body is a boudoir body.

“I’ll wait until I lose weight.”

I’ve watched women wait for a body that keeps moving. The ones who came anyway all say the same thing: I wish I’d stopped waiting sooner.

“Who else will be there?”

Just you and me. Hair and makeup happens at the salon beforehand, and the studio has private rooms for every part of your day.

“What if someone sees my photos?”

Your images are yours. Nothing is ever posted to my website or social media without your written permission. Wardrobe malfunctions? Totally normal — we fix it, laugh and move on.

Good to know

Your questions, answered

Which dates are included?

Every remaining session date on my calendar through December 31, 2026. I photograph on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and the list above shows what’s still open right now.

How long is this special running?

Until the dates are gone. Once every 2026 opening is booked, this round is over.

What does the $99 retainer cover?

It reserves your date and covers your consultation call, professional hair and makeup, private use of the studio, the client wardrobe, 60 minutes of photography with full posing coaching, and your private reveal appointment. It’s normally $697.

How does the discount work?

You choose your collection when you book. Full Collections (Artist, Diamond, Gold and Silver) are 40% off and start at $2,040. The Digital Gallery is 40% off too, at $3,000. Petite Collections (Petite 15, 8 and 3) are $500 off and start at $750.

When does my package need to be paid?

In full, 30 days before your session date, on an interest-free payment plan (weekly, biweekly or monthly). If your date is less than 30 days away, your package is paid in full when you book.

Can I upgrade at my reveal?

Absolutely. If you can’t narrow it down (most women can’t!), you can move up to a larger collection right there at your reveal.

I’ve never done anything like this. Is that okay?

YES. Most of my clients have never been in front of a camera like this. I coach you through every single pose and expression.

Can I bring a friend?

I don’t allow guests for any part of your session or your ordering appointment. What I do is best delivered one to one, with your complete attention.

Anything I should skip before my session?

Please skip the spray tan — I don’t correct tan lines or spray-tan skin tone. No props, and clients must be 18 or older.

Where is the studio?

Just outside Jefferson City, Missouri: over 2,000 square feet built for boudoir, with a hair and makeup room, a wardrobe room and multiple sets.
